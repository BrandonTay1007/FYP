Feel free to edit as necessary based on project - not all items may be present in your topic. 

Intro material (as per mid-sem pitch)

Problem/Opportunity statement leading to the proposed project overview + deliverables - WHY the project exists and WHAT it solves/achieves

Target end users - WHO will use it

Project Requirements/Goals - WHAT is it expected to do etc finalised requirements (for now) should be presented here. There is no fixed/minimum number of requirements, but it should be substantial and uniquely different enough to require support and effort from all team members

Project Limitations/Scope - WHAT is it not capable of doing etc finalised limitations (for now)

Project Plan - updated/correct Gantt/RTM fixing what was wrong in mid-sem OR repeat if no errors - HOW the project will be executed and WHEN are the deliverables scheduled

Project design - software designs as part of the proposed solution, i.e. suggested UI prototype/wireframe design as minimum for all teams (see attachment below) plus any additional software design if needed

Project risk analysis - WHAT is anticipated may occur and HOW will it affect progress and WHAT possible actions can be taken to avoid/alleviate/recover if it occurs. You may show a risk register here if needed.

Conclusion - key points on what the team has discovered about (1) teamwork and/or (2) project management activities thus far and what does the team anticipate would be possible issues on these two points (i.e. team problems, management problems and NOT project risks)
