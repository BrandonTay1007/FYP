Dear all

To accommodate the topic variety running in MUM and to ensure equality among all the teams, the presentations by each team at the end of the semester should cover the following content with the suggested flow. Feel free to edit as necessary based on project - not all items may be present in your topic.

1. Intro material (as per mid-sem pitch)

8. Project risk analysis - WHAT is anticipated may occur and HOW will it affect progress and WHAT possible actions can be taken to avoid/alleviate/recover if it occurs. You may show a risk register here if needed.
9. Conclusion - key points on what the team has discovered about (1) teamwork and/or (2) project management activities thus far and what does the team anticipate would be possible issues on these two points (i.e. team problems, management problems and NOT project risks)

Hi, just wanted to ask what's the difference between Preliminary project requirements / objectives and Project Goals? 

To my understanding Preliminary project requirements is what we planned with the supervisor and what we plan to use. But isn't that the same thing as project goals which is our deliverables?

#### Comment/Reply (depth: 1)
**Author:** Soo Wooi King (staff)
**Posted:** 2026-09-03T15:08:30.308644+10:00
**[STAFF ANSWER]**

Although there is a large overlap between both, we tend to favour the following distinction between them where requirements = what you need to do to satisfy goals and goals = what you need to deliver (i.e. deliverables). Goals are often more general/overview-ish whilst requirements will often be more detailed and may breakdown to be task oriented.

Example

Goal : deliver online web portal for researched to monitor their grant application progress

Requirements : login/logout functions, create account functions, reporting functions, application for grant functions, etc
